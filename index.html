sendtally

A climbing log with effort tracking. See what six months of climbing adds up to.

WebiOSAndroid
01Easy session logging
Date, times, indoor or outdoor, and the climbs. V-scale or Font grades, sends and attempts, tries. About a minute, right after you climb.
02Session RPE for effort tracking
Every session is scored 1 to 10 against your own last eight weeks, so a big night reads as a big night. Not just a list of grades.
03Long term trends
Volume, grade pyramid, hardest send, flash rate and average grade, week by week. If you have been stuck at V5 since March, the chart says V5 since March.
04Sort and filter by tag
Tag sessions and climbs however you like, then sort and filter the log and the trends by them. Indoor, outdoor, a board, a project, a gym.
05Sync on Strava
Each logged session becomes one Rock Climbing activity with duration, sends, attempts and grades filled in. Strava sync is never paywalled.

Sendtally features:

Kept per climb
Grade in V-scale or Font, send or attempt, tries, and the climb’s name if you give it one.
Effort
Every session scored 1 to 10 against your own rolling eight weeks. Set it yourself or leave it on auto.
Trends
Volume, grade pyramid, hardest send, flash rate and average grade, weekly or all-time. The membership half, five dollars a month, which is what pays for the server.
Strava
Optional and free for everyone. One Rock Climbing activity per session, fingerprinted so it never doubles up.
Imports
None. Sessions are entered by you. sendtally does not import data from board apps or any other third party.
Platforms
Web, iOS and Android. The same account and the same log on all three.
Leaving
Delete your account and every session and token goes with it the same day.
